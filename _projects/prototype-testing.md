title: Compare & contrast prototype testing
order: 3
tag: Prototype testing
cover_image: ""
illustration: prototypes
summary: A media site was redesigning a key page and had four layout options. I tested which one helped people understand the page best.
role: Research lead
methods: Comparative testing, task-based prompts
team: Design
challenge_title: Preference isn't evidence
challenge: Asking people which design they like tells you about taste. The team needed to know how each layout shaped what people expected to find.
approach_title: Watch how people read the page
approach: I used open-ended tasks and observed attention, interpretation, and intent across all four versions, then compared patterns across participants.
impact:
  - Clear visual hierarchy proved more important than visual appeal alone.
  - Gave the design team behavioral evidence for the final layout.
  - Introduced a repeatable way to test early visual design.
