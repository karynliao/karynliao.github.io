---
title: Redefining online home décor shopping
order: 1
tag: Generative research
cover_image: /assets/images/cases/home-decor.svg
illustration: journey
summary: A home improvement retailer launched a new home décor category online. I reframed a request for small UI fixes into foundational research on how a new kind of customer shops for furniture.
role: Research lead
methods: Contextual interviews, co-synthesis workshops
team: Product, design, engineering
challenge_title: Solve the right problem first
challenge: Polishing the existing site would have meant retrofitting patterns built for a different shopper. The better question was what a great décor shopping experience looks like for this new audience.
approach_title: Follow the whole journey
approach: I interviewed recent furniture buyers in their homes, from inspiration through purchase and afterward. Stakeholders observed sessions and synthesized findings with me, which built shared ownership of the results.
impact:
  - Shifted the team toward designing around customers from the start.
  - Informed navigation, content, and product page direction.
  - Product visits and engagement increased after implementation.
  - Made research a strategic input to the roadmap.
